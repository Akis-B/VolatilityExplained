Purpose of this Project

The markets have been swinging wildly, making 2023 one of the most turbulent years in recent memory. For many investors, it can feel overwhelming to stay on top of every shift and development. This site was created to help you understand how volatility impacts your portfolio, market, and the opportunities within it.
